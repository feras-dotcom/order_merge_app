How it works

Repeat orders are checked before anything changes.

  1. 01

    A customer places more than one order.

    During a drop, a preorder, or a live sale, the same customer often checks out twice before the first order ships.

  2. 02

    MergeShip detects the repeat.

    Open, paid, unfulfilled orders from the same customer inside your combine window are flagged as candidates.

  3. 03

    Every candidate is checked before anything changes.

    Customer, shipping address and method, currency, and transferable items all have to match. Anything MergeShip cannot verify stays exactly as it was.

  4. 04

    Eligible items are combined before fulfillment.

    Items move into the oldest eligible order, the extra order is cancelled and closed, and one order is left to fulfill.

Frequently asked questions

Questions before you install?

Clear answers about what MergeShip changes and what it leaves alone.

What orders can MergeShip combine?+

MergeShip looks for paid, open, unfulfilled repeat orders from the same customer within the combine window. The customer, shipping address and method, currencies, and transferable item checks must pass. If a required check does not pass, the orders remain unchanged.

What happens to the second order?+

Its items are added to the oldest eligible order. When the combine completes, the extra order is cancelled and closed so the primary order is the one left to fulfill.

Does MergeShip automatically refund duplicate shipping charges?+

No. MergeShip does not automatically issue a refund when it cancels the extra order, including a refund for duplicate shipping charges. Any customer refund should be handled separately by the merchant.

What happens when MergeShip can't verify that a combine is safe?+

Nothing changes. MergeShip leaves the orders alone for the merchant to review or fulfill separately.

Can merchants control when automatic combining is enabled?+

Yes. Automatic combining can be turned on or off, and merchants can set the combine window that fits their workflow.

Is MergeShip useful for product drops and preorders?+

Yes. These concentrated buying windows often lead the same customer to place more than one order before fulfillment, which is exactly the repeat order pattern MergeShip is built to check.
